Team17 and their original authors. Extracted from the source/data already in
this repository; original notices and terms remain in effect.

AB3D II modelling references:
https://github.com/mheyer32/alienbreed3d2/tree/86abb5c5b28cbab874e46b96c94b93b51bff56ec/media/vectobj

and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in reconstruction.json. Hidden source meshes
in the .blend are reference-only; exported meshes are new reconstructions.
