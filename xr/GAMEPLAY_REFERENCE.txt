Observed reference behavior and source checks:
- The first view looks into the courtyard, with recognizable panels, switches,
  indicator lamps and solid polygon medipacks/crates. Original ObjDraw3 checks
  object byte 6 == 0xFF and dispatches PolygonObj, not BitMapObj.
- Attacks and kills have bright impact sprites/gibs; incoming damage briefly
  tints the view red. USEPLR damage already sets hitcol in the C simulation,
  but the previous XR overlay never displayed it.
- Gameplay has generous target selection and distance-dependent hit rolls;
  the original has no free vertical look. PlayerShoot.s and CalcPLR1InLine,
  not video pixels alone, define the retained weapon rules. See ORIGINAL_TIMING.txt.
- Original view depth is dx*sin + dz*cos, with +Z forward at initial angle 0
  (renderer.c rotate_one_point; AB3DI.s RotatePoints). The GPU view previously
  used that positive depth as OpenGL Z, putting the intended view behind the
  camera. The 512px XR fixture showed a wall at spawn instead of the courtyard.

Implemented corrections:
- Reflect original world Z once in the GPU view. The same inverse conversion
  now drives eye collision queries, controller muzzle positions, shooting rays,
  head-oriented movement and laser clipping. Virtual right turns follow the
  original positive engine angle; sprite facing accounts for reflected handedness.
  This corrects the initial view without altering level positions or save layout.
- Render the original ten PolygonObj model slots, their animation frames,
  UVs and TextureMaps/OldTexturePalScaled materials as cached GPU geometry and
  textures. 0xFF props no longer become 255-unit false billboard sprites.
- Respect authored wall strip offsets, horizontal masks, vertical texture phase
  and repeat scale. Type-13 see-through walls alpha-test palette index zero.
- XR body wall collision uses a swept circle and sliding on solid boundaries
  and closed portals, retaining original open-door, step and room transitions.
  Eye safety fading remains separate from locomotion and does not move tracking.
- Original sprite palette level zero is decoded once, with brightness applied
  on GPU. Emissive projectiles no longer inherit an arbitrary middle-dark palette;
  short palettes work, invalid animation indices use the original first-frame
  fallback, and failed decodes are cached instead of retried each frame.
- Show the original hitcol fadedown as a restrained red world tint beneath the
  readable stereo HUD. Existing shot pools and original explosion/gib art remain
  the effect sources.
- Hitscan damage is independent of cosmetic impact-slot availability. Impact
  artwork is valid on its creation frame. The original distance-dependent random
  hit roll and weapon cadence remain in place.
- Tracked projectiles use the full controller direction and existing gun speed,
  rather than the inherited TKG mouse-look vertical clamp. Swept hit detection
  intersects horizontal and vertical time intervals and resolves the earliest
  3D contact, including vertical shots. A read-only wall query prevents generous
  collision radii from reaching through solid boundaries. Impacts use contact Y.
- A short (maximum 1.25 m) depth-tested laser projects from the named muzzle
  socket. It shares the shot origin/direction and clips against live walls and
  floor/ceiling openings. It is hidden in the pause menu or at a blocked muzzle.
  This is a visual guide: it does not magnetize shots or select an aim-assist target.
  Grenades retain their ballistic gravity, so the line shows initial barrel aim.

Validation:
- All 16 levels load/render on the actual offscreen GPU with original polygon
  objects present. Stereo fixtures check laser draws in both eyes, effects,
  head-relative movement, smooth/snap turning, height reset, save/load and wall fade.
- A separate GPU check renders every animation frame of all ten original vector
  models, checks visible textured pixels and captures artifacts/vector-gpu-test/.
- Collision tests cover repeated sliding/retreat, open and closed portals,
  live doors, head clearance and standing/low-ceiling calibration.
- Production shooting tests cover damage with a full impact pool, steep launch
  direction, 3D crossing/missing segments, actual damage and display-independent
  weapon pacing. Tests also check emissive palette decoding and muzzle transforms.
- Build and CTest logs: artifacts/gameplay-fidelity-{build,tests}.log.
  Additional 512px-per-eye inspection: artifacts/gameplay-review/.

Remaining comparison work:
Curved walls, sky/water/light-beam effects, exact original polygon shading and
every enemy/weapon/level combination are not yet at complete visual parity.
Physical headset testing is still required for collision comfort, perceived
prop scale, laser visibility and practical aiming. No active desktop or display
manager was restarted, and no headset test was launched by these automated checks.
